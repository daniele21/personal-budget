## Scope

- Preserve native payment notification alerts and process-local diagnostic counters when the same owner is registered again during normal authentication callbacks.
- Keep the account-isolation boundary: real owner transitions still purge owner-scoped native candidates/settings, clear old alerts and reset diagnostics.
- Count callbacks from the allowlisted supported app packages *before* the effective listener and source-selection gate, without reading unselected notification content.
- Distinguish callbacks rejected because of an inactive listener generation from callbacks rejected because native owner/source settings do not allow processing.
- Report successful/failed active-notification snapshot reads and aggregate counts of supported/selected notifications observed through those snapshots.
- Preserve the existing offline candidate extraction, rule engine, idempotent persistence, Android listener recovery, explicit payment confirmation, and privacy boundary. No new permission, persistent telemetry, notification payload, merchant, amount, key, per-package/timestamp report or network transfer.

## Release notes — English

Improved private on-device payment detection diagnostics. Aura now distinguishes
supported payment-app notifications received by the native listener from those
rejected by its filters, and reports whether supported notifications are visible
in Android's active-notification snapshots. Repeat sign-ins for the same account
no longer clear existing Aura payment alerts or diagnostic counters. Android/OEM
delivery blackouts on physical devices are **not yet confirmed fixed**.

## Real-environment diagnosis remains open

After updating from the Play test track *without uninstalling Aura or resetting
notification access*, first verify the supported bank/wallet apps are still
selected. When a payment-app notification visibly appears in the Android shade
but no Aura alert appears, inspect **More → Payments to review → Detection
controls → Local detector diagnostics** and refresh.

Relevant counters:

- **Supported-app callbacks before Aura filters**: known source callback delivered to either listener generation.
- **Rejected: inactive listener generation**: callback discarded by Aura's effective-generation gate.
- **Rejected: account or source selection**: callback discarded by owner/settings gate before extras are read.
- **Successful active-notification snapshots** and **Failed active-notification snapshots**: OS snapshot capability, not proof of live callback delivery.
- **Supported notifications visible in snapshots** and **Selected notifications visible in snapshots**: cumulative observations, not unique payments and not per-package identifiers.
- **Selected Android callbacks**, **Notifications processed**, **Exact payments**, **Payments needing review**, **Ignored by rules**, **Candidates created**, **Aura alerts posted / not posted** and **Processing failures** show later stages.

All counters remain process-local and reset after process death/restart or an
actual account boundary. An all-zero result does **not** prove whether Android
failed to deliver the bank notification, Android notification filters excluded
it, or the runtime bank app has a package name absent from Aura's allowlist.
The signed physical-device test, notification shade evidence, selected-source
status and (where authorized) device-side Android diagnostics are required to
identify that cause.

Do not claim reliable real-world bank notification delivery, a complete
blackout fix, or clearance of the outstanding M9/privacy/physical-device pilot
gates from successful CI or Google Play internal publication.
