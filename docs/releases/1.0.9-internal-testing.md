# Aura 1.0.9 (12) — Internal Testing

## Release name

`Aura 1.0.9 (12) — Payment detection reliability`

## Track

Google Play Internal Testing only. Payment detection remains beta-only and this
build must not be promoted to production while the privacy, physical-device and
whole-app release gates remain open.

## Google Play release notes — English

Improved Android payment detection reliability. Aura now distinguishes granted
notification access from a live notification-listener connection, retries the
system listener binding when needed, accepts silent payment notifications from
selected supported apps, and recovers its local candidate database correctly
after logout or reset. The payment review and confirmation flow is unchanged.

## Note di rilascio Google Play — Italiano

Migliorata l'affidabilità del rilevamento pagamenti su Android. Aura ora
distingue il permesso notifiche dalla connessione effettiva del listener,
richiede un nuovo binding al sistema quando necessario, gestisce anche le
notifiche di pagamento silenziose delle app supportate selezionate e riapre
correttamente il database locale dei candidati dopo logout o reset. Il flusso
di revisione e conferma dei pagamenti non cambia.

## Scope and rollback

- No Room schema migration is introduced.
- No new network service, analytics path, processor or subprocessor is added.
- Notification content remains package-gated before extras are read and raw
  notification text is not persisted.
- Rollback: halt the Internal Testing rollout and upload a replacement artifact
  with a higher `versionCode`; testers can pause payment detection and revoke
  notification access in Android settings.
- Physical/OEM validation and the existing privacy, security and production
  gates remain open.
