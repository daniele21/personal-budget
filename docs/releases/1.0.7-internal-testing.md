# Aura 1.0.7 (10) — Internal Testing

## Release name

`Aura 1.0.7 (10) — PayPal detection beta`

## Track

Google Play Internal Testing only. This build includes the beta-only Android
notification listener and must not be promoted to production while the
core-only production gate remains in force.

## Google Play release notes — English

Payment detection now supports PayPal purchase notifications, alongside Google
Wallet and Intesa Sanpaolo Mobile. PayPal payments are detected locally and
always require your review before becoming transactions. Aura now also warns
before creating a possible duplicate detected by another app or already present
in your transactions. This update includes account-deletion improvements,
deterministic transaction import hardening, and Android reliability fixes.

## Internal release notes — Italian

- Aggiunta la detection locale delle notifiche di acquisto PayPal in EUR.
- I titoli PayPal completi possono precompilare l'esercente; quelli troncati
  restano in revisione senza inferenze.
- Ogni pagamento rilevato richiede conferma esplicita prima di diventare una
  transazione.
- I possibili duplicati cross-app o già presenti nel ledger sono segnalati e
  richiedono una conferma esplicita prima di mantenere entrambi.
- Incluse le sorgenti beta Google Wallet e Intesa Sanpaolo Mobile.
- Inclusi miglioramenti alla cancellazione account, all'import deterministico
  CSV/XLSX e all'affidabilità Android.

## Scope and rollback

- No schema or persisted-data migration.
- No new network service, analytics path, processor, or subprocessor.
- Rollback: halt the Internal Testing rollout and replace the artifact with a
  higher `versionCode`; pending payment candidates can be purged from Aura and
  notification access can be revoked in Android settings.
- Physical-device PayPal validation and the existing privacy, security, Play,
  and production gates remain open.
