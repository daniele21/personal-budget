# Aura 1.0.17 — Internal Testing

## Release name

`Aura 1.0.17 — Local category memory`

## Track

Google Play Internal Testing only. Payment detection remains beta/internal.

## Scope

- Detected payment reviews can prefill a category from Aura's existing local
  transaction history when the recognized merchant has one consistent active
  category.
- Merchant matching remains conservative: case, accents, punctuation and
  whitespace are normalized, while merchant words are not removed or guessed.
- Conflicting history, inactive categories and missing merchants leave category
  selection manual.
- Suggestions remain editable and require the existing explicit transaction
  confirmation.
- No merchant-category rule, confidence score, suggestion metadata, new storage
  schema, network call, Firebase path, Harnex call or AI categorization is
  introduced.
- Existing Android payment-listener health V2 and its recovery behavior remain
  unchanged.

## Release notes — English

Aura can now remember how you categorized previous purchases from the same
recognized merchant. When your local history is consistent, the category is
prefilled for review and can still be changed before saving. Conflicting or
missing history remains manual.

## Note di rilascio — Italiano

Aura ora può ricordare come hai categorizzato gli acquisti precedenti dello
stesso esercente riconosciuto. Se lo storico locale è coerente, la categoria
viene precompilata nella schermata di revisione e resta modificabile prima del
salvataggio. In caso di storico conflittuale o assente, la scelta resta manuale.
