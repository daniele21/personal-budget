# Aura 1.0.14 — Internal Testing

## Release name

`Aura 1.0.14 — Payment detection reliability`

## Track

Google Play Internal Testing only. Payment detection remains beta/internal.

## Scope

- Harden Android payment-notification listener recovery after process recreation
  and reboot.
- Recover provider-managed Room storage after logout/reset purge without
  retaining a stale closed database.
- Accept supported silent payment notifications.
- Surface listener reconnection state truthfully in the UI.
- Restore a green browser baseline by following the first-run backup-check
  contract and fixing the payment inbox banner contrast issue.

## Release notes — English

Improved Android payment detection reliability, including recovery after
background process recreation and reboot. Aura also improves notification
listener status feedback and fixes first-run and accessibility issues found by
the automated browser suite.

## Note di rilascio — Italiano

Migliorata l'affidabilità del rilevamento pagamenti su Android, incluso il
recupero dopo la ricreazione del processo in background e il riavvio del
dispositivo. Migliorati anche lo stato del listener notifiche, il primo avvio e
alcuni problemi di accessibilità rilevati dai test automatici.
