# Aura 1.0.16 — Internal Testing

## Release name

`Aura 1.0.16 — Payment listener health V2`

## Track

Google Play Internal Testing only. Payment detection remains beta/internal.

## Scope

- Replace the assumption that Android `listenerConnected=true` means the
  notification listener is healthy.
- Probe and reconcile the effective listener while it is still connected, with
  foreground, WorkManager and best-effort in-process heartbeat recovery.
- Recover selected active payment notifications whose posted callback was
  missed, then cycle the listener binding when missed-callback evidence is
  confirmed.
- Introduce `AuraNotificationListenerServiceV2` with a fresh Android component
  identity and all supported notification filter types, avoiding inherited
  legacy alerting-only filter state.
- Retire the legacy listener after V2 ownership is established and keep late
  legacy callbacks inert while Android completes teardown.
- Keep a stable recovery window so a newer callback cannot move past an older
  missed payment; retain bounded replay and repository idempotency.
- Repeat active-notification reconciliation at 1, 3 and 7 seconds after a
  successful bind because Android can report the listener live before its active
  snapshot has finished populating.
- Expose explicit one-time listener upgrade and repair actions in payment
  detection settings.

## Release notes — English

Improved Android payment detection reliability for long-running installs. Aura
now verifies listener health even when Android still reports it connected,
recovers eligible active payment notifications after missed callbacks or
reconnects, and uses a new listener identity to avoid legacy notification-filter
state. Reconnect recovery also retries briefly while Android finishes restoring
the active-notification snapshot.

## Note di rilascio — Italiano

Migliorata l'affidabilità del rilevamento pagamenti Android nelle installazioni
di lunga durata. Aura ora verifica la salute del listener anche quando Android
lo indica ancora come connesso, recupera le notifiche di pagamento idonee dopo
callback perse o riconnessioni e usa un nuovo listener per evitare vecchi filtri
Android persistenti. Dopo una riconnessione Aura ripete inoltre per pochi
secondi la verifica mentre Android completa il ripristino delle notifiche attive.

## Remaining real-environment evidence

Automated qualification covers the deterministic Android paths, including the
specific reconnect race that previously failed intermittently in CI. It does not
replace prolonged physical/OEM evidence. Keep 1.0.16 installed normally on the
target phone, do not reinstall Aura or manually reset notification access, and
verify real payment notifications over multiple background periods. If a payment
notification is removed from Android before any listener recovery snapshot can
see it, active-notification reconciliation cannot reconstruct notification
history that Android no longer exposes.
