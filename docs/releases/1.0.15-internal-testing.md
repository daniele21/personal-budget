# Aura 1.0.15 — Internal Testing

## Release name

`Aura 1.0.15 — Payment listener self-healing`

## Track

Google Play Internal Testing only. Payment detection remains beta/internal.

## Scope

- Add a native WorkManager watchdog that retries Android notification-listener
  binding while payment detection remains enabled.
- Recover selected active payment notifications that arrived during a listener
  blackout when the listener reconnects.
- Keep recovery bounded by an owner-scoped monotonic watermark, a 128-item cap,
  and the existing 14-day candidate horizon.
- Preserve package/user-selection gating before notification extras are read and
  reuse existing technical fingerprints for idempotent replay.
- Distinguish Android listener health from Aura's own payment-alert notification
  channel and expose dedicated repair actions in the UI.
- Cancel background listener-watchdog work when detection is paused or native
  payment state is purged.

## Release notes — English

Improved Android payment detection reliability with a native self-healing
listener watchdog. Aura can now recover eligible active payment notifications
that arrived during a listener blackout after Android reconnects the listener,
while preserving on-device filtering and deduplication. Payment settings also
show separate repair actions for notification access and blocked Aura alerts.

## Note di rilascio — Italiano

Migliorata l'affidabilità del rilevamento pagamenti su Android con un watchdog
nativo che ripristina il listener notifiche. Dopo la riconnessione Aura può
recuperare le notifiche di pagamento idonee ancora attive arrivate durante un
blackout del listener, mantenendo filtro locale e deduplicazione. Le impostazioni
mostrano inoltre azioni distinte per riparare l'accesso notifiche e il canale
degli avvisi Aura.
