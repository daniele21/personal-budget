# Aura 1.0.18 — Internal Testing

## Release name

`Aura 1.0.18 — Payment detector diagnostics`

## Track

Google Play Internal Testing only. Android payment detection remains beta/internal.
This is not a general production rollout.

## Scope

- Distinguish **Connected** (listener bound to Android) from proof of actual payment callback reception; do not label a merely bound listener **Active**.
- Explicitly show **No apps selected** when no supported payment source has been selected.
- Display opt-in-on-screen, aggregate, process-local listener diagnostics: selected callback count, active-notification replay attempts, processed envelopes, EXACT/REVIEW/IGNORED rule outcomes, candidate creation/update/duplicate outcomes, Aura alert attempts/results and processing failures.
- Offer **Refresh diagnostics** to retrieve an updated native status even when no candidate events were created.
- Reset diagnostic counters at native account registration/purge; no financial content, merchant, amount, notification key, package identifier or timestamp is persisted or transmitted as diagnostic telemetry.
- Keep native privacy gates, supported sources, rule matching, recovery behavior and the explicit transaction-confirmation workflow unchanged.

## Release notes — English

Added private, on-device payment detector diagnostics for beta testers. Aura now
distinguishes an Android listener that is merely connected from one for which
payment callbacks have been observed. A source-selection warning and a manual
diagnostics refresh make silent payment detection failures easier to investigate.
The update does not claim to fix all Android/OEM listener delivery blackouts.

## Note di rilascio — Italiano

Aggiunta la diagnostica locale del rilevamento pagamenti per i tester interni.
Aura distingue ora il listener Android collegato dalla ricezione effettiva delle
notifiche. Un avviso segnala quando non è selezionata alcuna app di pagamento
e un aggiornamento manuale dei contatori aiuta a individuare il punto in cui
si perdono i pagamenti. Questo aggiornamento non garantisce ancora la
risoluzione dei blackout del listener sui dispositivi reali.

## Remaining real-environment evidence

Internal publication and deterministic CI do **not** establish that all real
Intesa Sanpaolo, Google Wallet and PayPal notifications arrive after prolonged
Android background/OEM restrictions. Test with normal bank notifications, without
reinstalling the app or repeatedly resetting Android access, and inspect
`Local detector diagnostics` before and after a missed notification.

If `Selected Android callbacks` does not increase after a bank notification,
investigate listener/OS delivery and verify the relevant app is selected. If
callbacks increase but candidates do not, inspect rules, duplicate and processing
counters; if candidates increase without visible Aura alerts, inspect alert
permission/channel and the alert counters. Diagnostic counters are process-local,
not a history of every payment, and active-notification recovery cannot reconstruct
notifications Android has already removed.
