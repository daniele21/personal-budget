## Scope

- Intesa Sanpaolo's offline EXACT rule `v3` now requires an explicit `Hai pagato` signal, a EUR amount, and a bounded identifiable merchant introduced by `da` or `presso`.
- Card suffix, date, time, card wording and their exact positions are no longer obligatory.
- Invalid or card/account-like merchants do not qualify for EXACT; such otherwise valid payment notifications remain REVIEW.
- Negative signals including declined/cancelled/not-paid transactions continue to take precedence.
- Redacted positive, near-miss, and negative parser fixtures and Kotlin tests added.
- Preserves on-device processing, local candidates, explicit confirmation, source-selection/ownership gates, existing deduplication and listener recovery. No generic REVIEW alerts or broad change to source acceptance.

Il parser dei pagamenti di Intesa Sanpaolo riconosce più varianti testuali senza richiedere data, ora e ultime cifre della carta in una posizione fissa. Il riconoscimento completo richiede comunque un pagamento esplicito, l'importo in euro e un esercente attendibile. ## Real-device follow-up

Automated success does **not** prove that every real Intesa notification format will be classified EXACT, nor that the intermittent Android/OEM notification delivery blackout has been fixed. Preserve the existing app installation and notification-access grant, verify the Intesa source is selected, then compare process-local counters before/after new payment notifications:

- `Supported-app callbacks` and `Selected Android callbacks` measure listener delivery.
- `Exact payments` and `Payments needing review` distinguish full vs partial parsing.
- `Candidates created/updated` and `Aura alerts posted/failed` reveal downstream behavior.

Do not interpret older pending REVIEW candidates as automatically reparsed by the update. Device-side verification, privacy and pilot readiness gates remain separate.
